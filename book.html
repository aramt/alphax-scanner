HTF Book

Weekly is the bull. Daily is the swing. Log entries, trims, reloads, and extra margin. Regime breaks on two weekly closes under the 100 DMA — not on daily noise. Stables come from trimming strength, not from selling weakness.

Leave qty blank: $2,000 margin at 2x at $0.25 → 16,000 coins, $4,000 notional.
Set a sync phrase so the book survives closing the browser and works on your phone.

Average entry is the live bag: sells lock realized PnL and leave remaining coins at the old average; buys blend a new average. Extra margin does not change entry — it lowers effective leverage and moves estimated liquidation down. Sync phrase stores the book on aramt.com (not in the URL, not in git).
